# Écran de salle Augny Badminton

Replacement for Badnet's "Matchs en cours + Point de rassemblement" hall screen (`/tournoi/ecran/mec-pdr`).

## Register
product

## Users
Players, coaches, officials and spectators in a sports hall during a badminton tournament. They glance up from 5 to 10 metres away, often between matches, in bright mixed gym lighting.

## Purpose
Answer two questions at a glance: what is being played on each court right now, and who must report to the assembly point next. It runs unattended all day on a landscape TV. Nobody clicks it.

## Brand
Augny Badminton. Club navy #0A1F44 and blue #0080F4, logo with a jumping player and a shuttle trailing blue swooshes. It works on any Badnet tournament by event id, always in Augny colours.

## Content
Court number, elapsed time, draw label, round, match number, both sides' players with age category, level and club, live set scores, umpire name, assembly list and next match number.

## Constraints
Legible from 10 metres. No interaction. Data from the ScorBad feed and Badnet screens. Design first, live wiring after approval. Courts vary from a few to 12 or more.

## Anti-references
Badnet's dense dark table of tiny text. Betting or esports aesthetics.
